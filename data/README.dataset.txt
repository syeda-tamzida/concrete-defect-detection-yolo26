## Data Availability

The data supporting the findings of this study are publicly available under the [CC BY 4.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The dataset contains annotated images of multiple concrete surface defects, including cracks, efflorescence, exposed reinforcement, rust stains, scaling, and spalling. Any additional data generated during this study, such as processed annotations or model outputs, can be obtained from the corresponding author upon reasonable request.
